### Legitimate interests (short assessment)

- **View statistics for link owners** (`sr_vs`, view rows): the owner shared the video to be watched and wants to
  know whether it was; the data is per-video, first-party, without IP storage, never used for ads or profiles;
  viewers expect a view count on a video page. Balance: favours processing, with the "Don't count my views" opt-out (O10).
- **Comments, reactions and blocks**: needed to show the comment the viewer chose to post and to protect owners
  from abuse. Balance: favours processing.
- **Security and abuse prevention** (rate-limit IP tags for a day, sign-in session IPs, hosting logs): short-lived,
  pseudonymous where possible. Balance: favours processing.
