# Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Snapreel, an iPhone app and website (snapreel.video) made by Arclayer Ltd, handles your information. The short answer: your recordings stay on your iPhone, unless you choose to share one as a link.

<!-- DRAFT for owner review: add Arclayer Ltd's registered address and company number, and confirm the minimum age (13 below). -->

## In short

- You can record, organise, transcribe and send files without an account. All of that stays on your iPhone.
- If you share a recording as a link, you need an account, and that recording is uploaded to snapreel.video until you stop sharing it.
- We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
- The people who watch your links are counted with an anonymous cookie, only on snapreel.video.

## What stays on your iPhone

Snapreel keeps the following on your device, in storage that only Snapreel can reach, protected by iOS data protection:

- your recordings and their thumbnails
- titles, folders, tags, notes and emoji reactions
- transcripts, and summaries where your iPhone can make them
- the list in the Notifications tab
- your settings and recent searches

We can't see any of it, and none of it is uploaded unless you share that recording as a link.

## Your account

You need an account only to share recordings as links or to subscribe to Snapreel Pro. You can sign in with Apple or with a code sent to your email address. We keep:

- your email address (with Sign in with Apple you can choose to share a private relay address instead)
- your name, if you give one or Apple shares it when you first sign in
- an account ID, and records of when you signed in
- your Snapreel Pro plan, its renewal date and Apple's transaction ID for it, if you subscribe
- a push notification token for each iPhone you're signed in on, so we can tell you about new viewers and comments

Your sign-in session is kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

## Recordings you share as links

When you tap Copy link, Snapreel uploads that one recording to snapreel.video: a copy of the video made for the web, a still picture and a 3-second preview, with its title, length, size and, if one was made on your iPhone, its transcript (used for captions on the watch page). We keep them, and your link's settings (password, expiry, downloads, comments, reactions and button), until you stop sharing the link or delete your account. A link that expires is deleted.

Anyone you send a link to can watch the recording, read its title and transcript, and see your name. Links aren't listed or searchable. A link with a password is shown only to people who type it.

## People who watch your links

The watch page at snapreel.video sets one first-party cookie, a random ID, so we can count views and unique viewers and keep a viewer's place. It isn't shared with anyone else and isn't used on other sites. For each view we record:

- roughly where the viewer is (their country, worked out from their internet address, which we don't keep)
- the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer) and the website they came from
- how much of the video they watched, and when
- a name, only if the viewer types one to comment or react

Viewers can comment and react, and report a video to us. If a viewer comments, their name and comment are shown to you and to other viewers. You can delete any comment on your videos and block the viewer who wrote it; a block is kept so that viewer can't comment or react on your videos again.

## Snapreel Pro and payments

Snapreel Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details; we never see them. Apple tells us which plan you bought and when it renews, changes, is refunded or ends, and we store that against your account. Cancellations and refunds are handled by Apple.

## Who processes your information

We use these companies to run Snapreel, under contracts that let them use your information only to provide their service to us:

- **Supabase** – our database, sign-in and video storage (United States).
- **Vercel** – hosts snapreel.video and its servers (United States and worldwide).
- **Resend** – sends our emails, such as sign-in codes and alerts about viewers and comments.
- **Apple** – Sign in with Apple, push notifications and App Store payments.

Some of them process information outside the UK, under safeguards such as the UK's International Data Transfer Agreement or Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Permissions

Snapreel asks for a permission only when a feature needs it. You can change your answer at any time in the Settings app.

- **Camera** – to record camera videos.
- **Microphone** – to record your voice with a recording.
- **Photos (add only)** – to save recordings you choose to your Photos library. When you import a video, you pick it in Apple's photo picker, which gives Snapreel only that video.
- **Speech recognition** – to make transcripts. Snapreel only uses recognition that runs on your iPhone; your audio is never sent anywhere to be transcribed.
- **Notifications** – to tell you when a recording is saved, a transcript is ready, space is running low, or someone watched or commented on one of your links.

Screen recording uses the broadcast feature built into iOS. You start and stop it yourself, and the recording is written straight into Snapreel's storage on your iPhone.

## The Lock Screen card

While you record your screen, Snapreel shows a card with the time on your Lock Screen. When a screen recording ends while Snapreel is in the background, Snapreel sends our server the card's push code (a random code Apple makes for that one card) and how long the recording ran, so Apple's push service can stop the card. The server passes them on and keeps nothing. Nothing about your recording is sent.

## Why we use your information

We use your information to provide Snapreel: to sign you in, host and show the recordings you share, count views, deliver comments and alerts, provide Snapreel Pro, keep the service secure and prevent abuse, and answer you when you contact us. Our legal bases are performing our agreement with you (the terms of use), our legitimate interest in keeping Snapreel safe and working, and our legal obligations.

## How long we keep it

<!-- DRAFT for owner review: confirm the log and support-email retention periods below. -->

- Shared recordings, their views, comments and reactions: until you stop sharing the link, it expires, or you delete your account.
- Your account and plan: until you delete your account. Records we must keep for tax or accounting are kept for as long as the law requires.
- Server logs, which include internet addresses: up to 30 days.
- Emails to support: until your question is resolved, and then for up to a year.

## Your choices and rights

- Stop sharing any link in its **Link settings**. Its video, views and comments are deleted from snapreel.video.
- Turn alerts about viewers and comments off in **Settings › Notifications**.
- Delete your account in **Settings › Account › Delete account**. This deletes your links and everything on snapreel.video, your sign-in, and revokes Sign in with Apple. It doesn't cancel an App Store subscription: cancel that in the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions.
- **Settings › Storage › Delete all my data** removes everything Snapreel keeps on your iPhone.

You can also ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

## Children

You must be 13 or older to create a Snapreel account. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you think a child has given us information, write to us and we'll delete it.

## Reporting a video

Anyone can report a shared video from its watch page, or by writing to [support@snapreel.video](mailto:support@snapreel.video). We review reports and remove videos that break our terms.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app or by email if the change matters.
