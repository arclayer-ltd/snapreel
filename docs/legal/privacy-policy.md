# Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Snapreel, an iPhone app made by Arclayer Ltd, handles your information. The short answer: it stays on your iPhone.

## In short

- No account and no sign-in. The only thing that ever reaches our server is a push code that stops the Lock Screen card, and it isn't kept.
- We don't collect, store, share or sell any information about you.
- No analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- Your recordings leave your iPhone only when you choose to send them.

## What stays on your iPhone

Snapreel keeps the following on your device, in storage that only Snapreel can reach, protected by iOS data protection:

- your recordings and their thumbnails
- titles, folders, tags, notes and emoji reactions
- transcripts, and summaries where your iPhone can make them
- the list in the Notifications tab
- your settings and recent searches

We can't see any of it. Snapreel goes online for one thing only: stopping the Lock Screen card when a screen recording ends (see below).

## Permissions

Snapreel asks for a permission only when a feature needs it. You can change your answer at any time in the Settings app.

- **Camera** – to record camera videos.
- **Microphone** – to record your voice with a recording.
- **Photos (add only)** – to save recordings you choose to your Photos library. When you import a video, you pick it in Apple's photo picker, which gives Snapreel only that video.
- **Speech recognition** – to make transcripts. Snapreel only uses recognition that runs on your iPhone. If that isn't available for your language, no transcript is made and your audio is never sent anywhere.
- **Notifications** – to tell you when a recording is saved, a transcript is ready or space is running low. These notifications are created on your iPhone; no notification service is involved.

Screen recording uses the broadcast feature built into iOS. You start and stop it yourself, and the recording is written straight into Snapreel's storage on your iPhone.

## The Lock Screen card

While you record your screen, Snapreel shows a card with the time on your Lock Screen. iOS can stop a screen recording while Snapreel is in the background (for example when you lock your iPhone), and only Apple's push service can then stop that card. So when a screen recording ends, Snapreel's screen recorder sends our server at snapreel.video two things: the card's push code (a random code Apple makes for that one card) and how long the recording ran. The server passes them straight to Apple's push service so the card stops counting, and keeps nothing. Like any web request it comes from your internet address, which our host, Vercel, may keep in routine logs for a short time. Nothing about your recording, its title or anything else in Snapreel is sent.

## When information leaves your iPhone

Only when you make it happen:

- **Sharing** – when you share a recording, it goes to the app or person you choose, and what happens next is up to them and that app.
- **Saving to Photos** – a copy is added to your Photos library, which syncs with iCloud Photos if you use it.
- **Backups** – if you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Snapreel's data is included and handled under Apple's terms.
- **Contacting support** – if you email us, we receive your email address, your message and, only if you choose to include them, your device details (model, iOS version, app version and free space). We use these only to answer you, and delete the conversation when it's no longer needed. Recordings are never attached for you.

## Children

Snapreel isn't aimed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect anything from anyone of any age.

## Deleting your information

Delete a recording to remove it along with its notes, reactions and transcript. **Settings › Storage › Delete all my data** removes all of it at once. Deleting the app removes everything Snapreel stored on your iPhone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and mention the change in the app's release notes.
