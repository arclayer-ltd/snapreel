Last updated: 20 November 2026

## In short

- You can record, organise, transcribe and send files without an account. All of that stays on your iPhone.
- If you share a recording as a link, you need an account, and that recording is uploaded to snapreel.video until you stop sharing it.
- We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.
- The people who watch your links are counted with a cookie that only snapreel.video uses.

## What stays on your iPhone

Snapreel keeps the following on your device, in storage that only Snapreel can reach, protected by iOS data protection:

- your recordings and their thumbnails
- titles, folders, tags, notes and emoji reactions
- transcripts, and summaries where your iPhone can make them
- the list in the Notifications tab
- your settings and recent searches

We can't see any of it, and none of it is uploaded unless you share that recording as a link. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include it, under Apple's own privacy policy.

## Your account

You need an account only to share recordings as links or to subscribe to Snapreel Pro. You can sign in with Apple or with a code sent to your email address. We keep:

- your email address (with Sign in with Apple you can choose to share a private relay address instead)
- your name, if you give one or Apple shares it when you first sign in; it's shown on your watch pages
- your account ID
- for each device you're signed in on, when you signed in, its internet address and its browser or device type, to keep your account secure
- your link settings, such as your colour and call-to-action button, and whether you want email alerts
- your Snapreel Pro plan, its renewal date and Apple's transaction ID for it, if you subscribe
- a push notification token for each iPhone you're signed in on, so we can tell you about new viewers and comments

Your sign-in session is kept in your iPhone's Keychain, or in a cookie when you use the web dashboard at snapreel.video.

## Recordings you share as links

When you tap Copy link, Snapreel uploads that one recording to snapreel.video: a copy of the video made for the web, a still picture and a 3-second preview, with its title, length, size and, if one was made on your iPhone, its transcript (used for captions on the watch page).

Anyone you send a link to can watch the recording, read its title and transcript, and see your name. Links aren't listed or searchable. A link with a password is shown only to people who type it.

## People who watch your links

Watching a link doesn't need an account. The watch page at snapreel.video sets a first-party cookie (`sr_vs`) that holds a random ID and lasts a year, so we can count views and unique viewers, keep a viewer's place, apply blocks and stop abuse. A link with a password sets a second cookie for 12 hours after you type the password. Neither is used for advertising, shared with anyone else, or read on other websites. The counts they give are statistics about each video, not a profile of you. You can block or delete these cookies in your browser and still watch; you'll just be counted as a new viewer each time, and a password link will ask for its password again. To stop your views being counted at all, turn on **Don't count my views** at the bottom of any watch page: it sets a small cookie (`sr_nocount`, for a year) and we then record no views from that browser, while `sr_vs` stays so that blocks and abuse limits still work.

For each view we record:

- roughly where the viewer is: their country, worked out from their internet address, which we don't store
- the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer) and the website they came from
- how much of the video they watched, and when
- a name, only if the viewer types one to comment or react, and their Snapreel account, if they're signed in

The link's owner sees view counts. With Snapreel Pro they also see each viewer's country, device and how far they watched, with the name the viewer typed or, for a viewer signed in to Snapreel, their account name.

To protect snapreel.video from spam and abuse, we also count requests per internet address. We store only a one-way code made from the address, never the address itself, and delete it within a day.

## Comments, reactions, blocks and reports

Viewers can comment and react if the owner allows it. A comment's name and text are shown to the owner and to everyone who can watch the link. The owner can delete any comment and block the viewer who wrote it; a block is kept so that viewer can't comment or react on the owner's videos again.

Anyone can report a video from its watch page. A report holds the reason, any details you write and, if you give it, your email address so we can reply. It's stored with the video and sent to our support mailbox.

## Snapreel Pro and payments

Snapreel Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details; we never see them. Apple tells us which plan you bought and when it renews, changes, is refunded or ends, and we store that against your account. Cancellations and refunds are handled by Apple.

## Emails and notifications

We email you sign-in codes and, unless you turn them off, alerts when someone watches, comments on or reacts to your links. Every alert has a link to turn alerts off. We don't send marketing email. Push notifications about your links go through Apple's push service, and you can turn them off in the Settings app or in **Settings › Notifications** in Snapreel.

## The Lock Screen card

While you record your screen, Snapreel shows a card with the time on your Lock Screen. When a screen recording ends while Snapreel is in the background, Snapreel sends our server the card's push code (a random code Apple makes for that one card) and how long the recording ran, so Apple's push service can stop the card. The server passes them on and keeps nothing. Nothing about your recording is sent.

## Permissions

Snapreel asks for a permission only when a feature needs it. You can change your answer at any time in the Settings app.

- **Camera** – to record camera videos.
- **Microphone** – to record your voice with a recording.
- **Photos (add only)** – to save recordings you choose to your Photos library. When you import a video, you pick it in Apple's photo picker, which gives Snapreel only that video.
- **Speech recognition** – to make transcripts. Snapreel only uses recognition that runs on your iPhone; your audio is never sent anywhere to be transcribed.
- **Notifications** – to tell you when a recording is saved, a transcript is ready, space is running low, or someone watched or commented on one of your links.

Screen recording uses the broadcast feature built into iOS. You start and stop it yourself, and the recording is written straight into Snapreel's storage on your iPhone.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account, profile and plan**: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes them, your shared videos and their files at once.
- **Sign-in records** (device, internet address): until you sign out on that device or delete your account.
- **Push tokens**: until you sign out on that iPhone, delete your account, or Apple tells us the token no longer works.
- **A shared recording and its views, comments and reactions**: while you share the link. When you stop sharing, they're deleted at once; we keep only the link's short code for 30 days, so the link says it was removed, and then delete that too.
- **A link that expires**: it stops playing on its expiry date. Its recording and activity are kept for 30 days in case you extend it, then deleted in the same way.
- **An upload that never finishes**: marked as failed after 48 hours, and its files are deleted 7 days later.
- **Rate-limit records** (a one-way code made from an internet address): up to 1 day.
- **The record of which alerts we've sent** (so you don't get the same one twice): 90 days.
- **Reports**: until the reported link is deleted, plus its 30-day removal notice.
- **Emails to support**: until your question is resolved, and then for up to a year.
- **Logs kept by our hosting providers**, which can include internet addresses: up to 30 days.
- **Viewer cookies**: up to a year in the viewer's browser (12 hours for a password cookie), or until the viewer clears them.

## Security

Information travels to and from snapreel.video over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and our providers encrypt it where it's stored. Recordings are kept in private storage, and each link is served through short-lived signed addresses. Link passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Only the developer has administrative access. No service is perfectly secure; if a breach puts you at risk, we'll tell you and the authorities where the law requires.

## Your choices and rights

- Stop sharing any link in its **Link settings**. Its video, views and comments are deleted from snapreel.video.
- Delete any comment on your videos, and block the viewer who wrote it.
- Turn alerts about viewers and comments off in **Settings › Notifications**, or with the link in any alert email.
- Delete your account in **Settings › Account › Delete account**, or on the web dashboard at snapreel.video. This deletes your links and everything on snapreel.video and your sign-in, and revokes Sign in with Apple. It doesn't cancel an App Store subscription: cancel that in the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions.
- **Settings › Storage › Delete all my data** removes everything Snapreel keeps on your iPhone.

Depending on where you live, you also have the right to ask us for a copy of your information (including in a portable format), to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to [support@snapreel.video](mailto:support@snapreel.video); we'll answer within a month and won't charge you. We may need to check that the request comes from you.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, write to the same address with "Privacy complaint" in the subject. We'll acknowledge it within 30 days and tell you what we did about it. You can also complain to a data protection authority at any time: in the EU, the authority in your country; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in Bangladesh, the authority under the Personal Data Protection Act 2026; elsewhere, the authority where you live.

## Children
